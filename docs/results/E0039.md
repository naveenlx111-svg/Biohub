# E0039: temporal 3D division-parent classifier

E0039 trains a small 3D CNN directly on previous/current/next image crops centered at sparsely annotated nodes. Positive examples are annotated nodes with at least two outgoing edges; negatives are observed continuing nodes with exactly one incoming and one outgoing edge. Evaluation holds out each embryo in turn.

This diagnostic tests image evidence directly instead of handcrafted summaries. It does not alter the tracking graph.

## Result

Breakthrough. The full training census produced 6,921 examples with 151 annotated division parents. Embryo-held-out mean average precision was `0.3571` and mean ROC AUC was `0.8101`. Held-out AP was `0.4878` on 44b6 and `0.2264` on 6bba.

The precision-recall curve contains usable operating points. A threshold near `0.99386` retained `20 TP / 15 FP`; the strictest audited threshold near `0.999964` retained `7 TP / 0 FP`. This is more than 400 times E0037's average precision and is the first parent classifier with a zero-observed-FP high-confidence region.

The result is not yet a submission because evaluation used crops centered at annotated ground-truth nodes. The next required audit must score detected parent proposals at their predicted coordinates and measure end-to-end graph rewiring with the official metric.

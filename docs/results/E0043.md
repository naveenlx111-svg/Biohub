# E0043: candidate-domain hard-negative fine-tuning

E0043 starts from each E0039 embryo-held-out raw temporal-crop model and adapts it using detector-centered proposals from the opposite embryo. Hard negatives are mined by the legal fold model itself; annotated training positives are retained as a stabilizer. Evaluation remains on the complete dense candidate pool of the held-out embryo.

Configuration: binary cross-entropy, 1,024 top hard negatives and 512 reservoir-sampled negatives per fold, 12 fine-tuning epochs, learning rate `2e-4`.

Status: completed and rejected. No graph mutation was performed.

## Result

The complete detected-parent pool contains 115,478 candidates and 16 labeled
positives. Candidate-domain BCE adaptation reached AP `0.004232` and ROC AUC
`0.603355`. The exact low-FP audit found `0 TP / 10 FP` in the global top ten
and `0 TP / 60 FP` across the top five candidates per movie. There is no
zero-FP true positive, and thresholds chosen on either embryo recover no true
positive on the other embryo.

E0043 fails every promotion gate. Its AP is above E0041's `0.003240`, but the
ranking head is worse and cannot support a safe graph edit.

## Promotion gate

The E0033 parent has division counts `5 TP / 9 FP / 13 FN`, so a clean repaired division raises division Jaccard by about `1/27 = 0.0370`, or roughly `0.0037` in the combined metric before its edge effect. Crossing `0.95` from the local `0.936854` parent requires approximately four clean additional division TPs. However, the current fifth-place frontier is tied at `0.950`; a safer `0.951` leaderboard target is `0.018` above our verified `0.933`, corresponding to roughly five clean repairs. E0043 is target-scale only if one frozen threshold obtains at least `5 TP` with at most `1 FP` across the complete twelve-movie candidate panel. A `4 TP / <=1 FP` result is still integration-worthy for crossing the local 0.95 proxy, while `2 TP / 0 FP` may justify a conservative leaderboard probe.

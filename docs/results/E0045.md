# E0045: wide non-wrapping localization augmentation

E0045 corrects two limitations in E0042. Training translations span two z voxels and nine y/x voxels (approximately `3.25–3.66 µm`), covering the observed missed-parent localization range, and use edge padding plus slicing rather than wraparound shifts.

## Result

The complete notebook ran locally on an RTX 5060 on 2026-09-01. The patched
official parent score reproduced E0033 to numerical precision at `0.93685422`
(`0.91833570` adjusted edge Jaccard and `0.18518519` division Jaccard; division
counts `5 TP / 9 FP / 13 FN`). The GT-aware oracle remained `0.97201732`.

- Annotated-centroid crop validation: AP `0.375340`, ROC AUC `0.793980`.
- Detected-parent transfer: `115488` proposals and `15` positive parent pairs.
- Candidate-domain AP: `0.015113`; ROC AUC: `0.751606`.
- Global top 10: `0 TP / 10 FP`.
- Top one per movie: `0 TP / 12 FP`.
- Top three per movie: `1 TP / 35 FP`.
- No non-empty threshold recovered a true positive at zero false positives.

The detected-domain AP is approximately `4.7x` E0041/E0042, so covering the
measured localization range with non-wrapping translations is a real ranking
improvement. It does not create the high-precision tail required for graph
mutation.

## Decision

Rejected for direct graph integration. Retain the wide-jitter score as a
candidate feature for a proposal-domain multi-view model, but do not add edges
from its absolute score or movie-relative rank alone.

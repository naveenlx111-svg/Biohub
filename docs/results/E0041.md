# E0041: temporal-crop transfer to detected parents

E0041 trains E0039's raw temporal 3D crop classifier with each embryo held out, then applies the held-out model to E0035's actual detected parent proposals at their predicted coordinates. Daughter pairs remain selected by the embryo-held-out geometry ranker.

This is the required domain-transfer audit between ground-truth-centered crops and deployable detected candidates. It does not yet alter graph edges.

## Result

- Annotated-node validation: AP `0.28713`, ROC AUC `0.77799`.
- Detected-parent transfer set: `115478` proposals containing `16` positive parent-pairs.
- Candidate-domain AP: `0.003240`.
- Candidate-domain ROC AUC: `0.71332`.
- Best audited strict region was still unusable: top `58` contained `1 TP / 57 FP`; top `12` contained `0 TP / 12 FP`.

## Decision

Rejected for graph integration. The temporal crop contains real division signal at annotated centroids, but direct application to the dense detected-parent proposal distribution loses the precision demonstrated by E0039. The next model must train against detected, candidate-centered hard negatives rather than only annotated continuing cells.

## Post-hoc complementarity audit

The complete `115478`-row E0041 output was joined one-to-one with E0037 track-context predictions. Embryo- and movie-relative percentile fusion improved ranking AUC but did not improve average precision over the crop score alone. At the diagnostically best movie-relative mixture, top 20 per movie produced `0 TP / 240 FP`, while top 50 per movie produced `5 TP / 595 FP`. Score fusion is therefore not a safe next graph mutation; candidate-domain training remains necessary.

# E0002: harmonic association weight 0.25

## Status

Running privately on Kaggle as `biohub-e0002-harmonic-w025`.

## Hypothesis

The reverse-time harmonic association signal is useful, but weight 0.30 is slightly too aggressive. The current public frontier reports 0.927 with weight 0.25, while E0001 at weight 0.30 produced only a small and unstable paired validation gain.

## Controlled change

- Parent: E0001
- Changed: `BIOHUB_BIDIRECTIONAL_EDGE_WEIGHT`, 0.30 to 0.25
- Unchanged: detector, model weights, node extraction, graph repair, division rules, and held-out samples

## Promotion rule

Promote only if the paired adjusted edge Jaccard improves over E0000-CV and the improvement is not driven by a single sample. The official patched division scorer must be used before claiming a combined-score gain.

## Research triage

Two newly published 195-volume analyses rule out broad parameter sweeps as the next priority:

- Node shedding has a maximum reward of `0.1 * edge_jaccard` per unit reduction in node ratio. On the comparable pipeline, short-track filters lost raw edge Jaccard at 1.3 to 3.3 times that hurdle. Only removing edgeless nodes is provably free.
- An eight-arm division geometry sweep produced no improvement over its baseline. Its reported baseline division Jaccard was 0.0767 with low precision, indicating that better division evidence is needed rather than wider geometric gates.

The next high-value direction after E0002 is confidence-gated edge recovery using temporal motion residual and forward/reverse agreement, evaluated on embryo-held-out folds.

# E0042: localization-robust crop transfer

E0042 repeats E0041's candidate-domain transfer audit with random training translations of up to one z voxel and four y/x voxels. These shifts approximate the measured localization difference between annotated centroids and detected parent proposals.

The experiment tests whether direct image evidence retains its high-precision region under realistic coordinate error. It does not mutate graph edges.

## Result

- Annotated-node validation: AP `0.30425`, ROC AUC `0.78973`.
- Detected-parent transfer set: `115478` proposals containing `16` positive parent-pairs.
- Candidate-domain AP: `0.003197`.
- Candidate-domain ROC AUC: `0.63569`.
- Top `58` contained `1 TP / 57 FP`; top `12` contained `0 TP / 12 FP`.

## Decision

Rejected. Localization jitter did not repair the deployment-domain gap and reduced candidate ROC AUC relative to E0041. The principal problem is dense proposal-domain hard negatives, not a small centroid offset alone.

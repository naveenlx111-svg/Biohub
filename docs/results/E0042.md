# E0042: localization-robust crop transfer

E0042 repeats E0041's candidate-domain transfer audit with random training translations of up to one z voxel and four y/x voxels. These shifts approximate the measured localization difference between annotated centroids and detected parent proposals.

The experiment tests whether direct image evidence retains its high-precision region under realistic coordinate error. It does not mutate graph edges.

## Result

- Annotated-node validation: AP `0.30425`, ROC AUC `0.78973`.
- Detected-parent transfer set: `115478` proposals containing `16` positive parent-pairs.
- Candidate-domain AP: `0.003197`.
- Candidate-domain ROC AUC: `0.63569`.
- Top `58` contained `1 TP / 57 FP`; top `12` contained `0 TP / 12 FP`.

## Decision

Rejected. Localization jitter did not repair the deployment-domain gap and reduced candidate ROC AUC relative to E0041. The principal problem is dense proposal-domain hard negatives, not a small centroid offset alone.

## Augmentation-radius audit

The corrected division-localization table shows that missed-division parent detections have median offset about `2.19 µm`, maximum `3.61 µm`, and `9/14` exceed `1.625 µm`. E0042 shifted at most one z voxel or four y/x voxels, both approximately `1.625 µm`, so it did not cover most measured missed-parent offsets. Its use of `np.roll` also wraps crop content at the borders. E0042 therefore rejects only this narrow wrapped-jitter implementation, not localization-robust image modeling generally. A future test should use non-wrapping shifts spanning roughly `3.6 µm` or explicit local-neighborhood pooling, while auditing the increased false-positive risk.

The complete E0041/E0042 candidate tables were also compared one-to-one. E0042 improved embryo-relative rank for `9/16` positive candidates and reduced their median rank from `7,666` to `3,329`; movie-relative median rank improved from `617` to `308.5`. One positive moved from movie rank `721` to `26`, while others were substantially demoted. Thus localization augmentation contains complementary signal but is unstable and does not create a usable high-precision tail. This paired result supports the corrected E0045 test without promoting E0042 itself.

Embryo-relative rank fusion of E0041 and E0042 increased diagnostic AP to `0.00739`, more than twice either individual rank score, confirming complementary centered and shifted views. Precision was still unsafe: the best audited top-two-per-movie selection produced only `1 TP / 23 FP`. Future localization modeling should learn or pool multiple views before calibration; a post-hoc E0041/E0042 ensemble must not mutate graphs.

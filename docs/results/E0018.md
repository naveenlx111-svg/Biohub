# E0018: division-aware reparenting

E0018 is the first production-changing experiment after the official division audit. Starting from SDW75, it searches for a detected second daughter that is currently assigned to another single-child track and moves that edge to a plausible dividing parent.

The repair is gated by consecutive time, parent and sister distance, two continuing daughter branches, outward divergence, a one-micron improvement over the old parent assignment, and a conservative global cap. Promotion requires an official combined-score gain without a material adjusted-edge regression.

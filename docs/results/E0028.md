# E0028: embryo-held-out fork ranker

E0028 consumes E0027's global candidates without rerunning image inference. A label-free geometry gate removes easy negatives while retaining every labeled positive, then a histogram gradient-boosted classifier is trained with each embryo held out in turn.

The primary diagnostics are positive top-one and top-three rank within each fork, embryo-held-out average precision, and the precision/recall trade-off after accepting at most one daughter pair per fork. This experiment does not mutate graphs and cannot produce a submission; it decides whether the available candidate features are adequate for E0029 integration.

## Result

The hard-negative gate reduced 511,908 proposals to 157,477 while retaining all 16 positive candidate triples. Under embryo-held-out prediction, the correct daughter pair ranked first within its proposed fork for `16/16` positives and therefore also ranked within the top three for `16/16`.

Global event discrimination failed. Mean average precision was only `0.000264`, despite ROC AUC `0.7146`. At the lowest audited threshold, four positives were accepted with 18,809 false candidates; higher thresholds retained at most one positive and then none.

The geometry-topology features can choose the correct daughter pair when the dividing parent is known, but they cannot determine which of roughly 119,000 parent-time candidates actually divides. E0029 must add parent-level image and temporal evidence and should preserve E0028's within-fork pair ranking rather than replacing it.

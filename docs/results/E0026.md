# E0026: global fork-candidate audit

E0026 freezes the 0.931 production anchor and tests a label-free global proposal generator before training a division ranker. For every detected node, it proposes pairs among the eight nearest next-frame detections within 7 µm, with a 12 µm sister-distance cap. Ground truth is used only afterward to measure whether the proposed set contains the oracle-repairable fork.

The experiment exports every candidate with geometry and graph-topology features plus per-division proposal recall. It is diagnostic and cannot be submitted. High recall with a manageable candidate count will justify training a calibrated classifier on these candidates; low recall will require a better proposal mechanism first.

## Result

The strict gate generated 60,832 candidates and proposed five of 18 annotated divisions. Conditional recall among the 15 divisions with matched role triples was `0.3333`. More importantly, it captured only two of the 11 oracle-rewired misses, although it retained three of four baseline true divisions.

This proposal is rejected as the ranker foundation: its smaller candidate set does not compensate for losing nine recoverable division events.

## Decision

Weight `0.30` produces a small positive aggregate edge delta but is unstable across samples. It is not strong enough to prioritize as the primary route to 0.95. Preserve it as an optional ensemble/association feature, but investigate adaptive gating or lower weights only after higher-value node-count calibration experiments.

The control validator's division number also uses obsolete connected-component logic and is not an official post-reset result.

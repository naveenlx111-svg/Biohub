# E0027: wide global fork-candidate audit

E0027 runs independently from E0026 with a wider proposal set: the twelve nearest next-frame detections within 10 µm, retaining the same 12 µm sister-distance cap.

This diagnostic measures the candidate-count cost of recovering divisions missed by E0026's stricter physical gate. Comparing E0026 and E0027 will select the smallest proposal space that preserves the oracle-repairable events before ranker training.

## Result

The wide gate generated 511,908 candidates and proposed 11 of 18 annotated divisions. Conditional recall among the 15 divisions with matched role triples was `0.7333`. It captured seven of the 11 oracle-rewired misses and all four baseline true divisions.

E0027 is the selected proposal foundation. Its recall is potentially sufficient for a material score gain, but the half-million-candidate search space requires aggressive hard-negative reduction and calibrated ranking before graph mutation.

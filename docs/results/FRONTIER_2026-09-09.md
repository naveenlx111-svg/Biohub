## Concrete TTA defect and next experiment

The advertised eight-view TTA contains seven unique transforms:
`rot90(image, 1).transpose(-1, -2)` is a horizontal flip, already present in the list.
The missing anti-diagonal reflection is `image.flip((-2, -1)).transpose(-1, -2)`.
Its inverse is transpose followed by flipping both axes.

`tools/true_d4_patch.py` verifies eight unique views, the inverse round-trip on a rectangular
test array, exactly two forward and three inverse replacements, and predictor compilation.
E0055 tests this single mechanism on E0053. A mathematically complete transform set is not
assumed to score better; it requires paired measurement.

# E0003: retain short tracks

## Status

Completed privately on Kaggle as `biohub-e0003-retain-short-tracks` and rejected.

## Result

Weighted adjusted edge Jaccard was **0.9149**, versus **0.9165** for E0000-CV: approximately **-0.0016**.

Retaining short components reduced missed annotated nodes from 40 to 22 and recovered 2,194 GT edges versus 2,186 in E0002. However, it raised predicted nodes from 97,657 to 101,120 across the four samples and increased fragmented GT edges from 56 to 69. The net adjusted score declined.

Per-sample adjusted edge Jaccards were 0.9277, 0.7972, 0.9774, and 0.8316. Only two samples improved relative to E0002, confirming that unconditional retention is not robust.

The displayed division Jaccard of 0.1250 is from the obsolete notebook proxy and is not used for promotion.

## Hypothesis

The frozen baseline discards every connected component shorter than six nodes unless it contains a predicted division. A recent 195-volume metric analysis measured the raw edge-Jaccard cost of comparable short-track shedding at 0.11 to 0.28 per unit of node ratio, above the approximately 0.086 hurdle imposed by the node-count adjustment. Reversing that filter may therefore recover more edge score than it loses through additional nodes.

## Controlled change

- Parent: E0000-CV
- Changed: `BIOHUB_OUTPUT_FILTER_SHORT_TRACKS`, enabled to disabled
- Unchanged: detector, both model weights, association, ILP, gap recovery, division rules, and validation samples

This arm retains connected short tracks but does not preserve isolated nodes because isolated-node pruning remains enabled.

## Decision rule

Compare against E0000-CV on identical samples using weighted adjusted edge Jaccard. Record the change in nodes, edge TP/FP/FN, and per-sample deltas. Promote only if the gain is not confined to one sample and runtime remains within budget.

## Follow-up if positive

If full retention helps but has uneven sample effects, replace the binary switch with a conservative rescue gate based on mean edge confidence, physical motion residual, and forward/reverse agreement. This would keep likely real short tracks while rejecting detector blips.

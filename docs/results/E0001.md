# E0001 — Harmonic bidirectional association, weight 0.30

Date: 2026-08-26

Status: completed, not submitted
Kaggle kernel: `naveenlx111249971939/biohub-e0001-harmonic-w030`, version 1

## Hypothesis

Reverse-time edge evidence combined with forward association probabilities through a weighted harmonic construction will suppress asymmetric false links while preserving the frozen 0.926 detector and post-processing pipeline.

## Controlled change

The experiment was constructed from the exact post-reset 0.926 notebook. Its inference cell was replaced with the public frontier cell after verifying that the only functional additions were:

- reverse-time primary edge prediction;
- forward/reverse logit alignment;
- harmonic probability fusion at weight `0.30`;
- a label-free detector-coordinate diagnostic manifest.

DeepCenter remained at epoch 500 (`checkpoint_last.pt`), safe-division DeepCenter gating remained disabled, the detection threshold remained `0.96875`, and all baseline tracking/post-processing configuration was preserved.

## Interpretation

The cross-sample spread is large (`0.7831` to `0.9771`). Node-count calibration is a visible weakness: the two lower-scoring samples over-predict the estimated node total, while `44b6_12dfb391` strongly under-predicts. A single global detection/retention policy is unlikely to be optimal.

The paired E0000-CV control scored `0.91655`; E0001 scored `0.91686`, an aggregate improvement of only `+0.00031`. Harmonic fusion helped two samples materially, was neutral on one, and harmed `44b6_267148e4` by `-0.01843`. This is a genuine but unstable edge gain.

## Decision

Do not prioritize or submit this arm yet. Preserve it as a possible gated association feature, but move the main research lane to sample-adaptive node-count/detection calibration. Any later promotion still requires patched official division scoring.

# Theoretical Research & Literature Foundations

This directory houses the team's theoretical research, mathematical formulations, biophysical modeling principles, and literature surveys for the Kaggle **Biohub - Cell Tracking During Development** research project.

## Contents

- **[`research_hurdles_and_theoretical_solutions.md`](research_hurdles_and_theoretical_solutions.md)**:
  Comprehensive analysis of the 5 core scientific hurdles (class imbalance, 4:1 optical anisotropy, metric topology, cytokinesis drift, and global ILP optimization) and their mathematical solutions based on foundational literature (Ultrack, Conservation Tracking, TGMM, LapTrack, TrackMate, and CTC/AOGM benchmarks).

## Guidelines for Theoretical Contributions

1. **Focus on Formal Mechanics**: Document explicit loss functions, cost matrices, linear constraints, and biophysical conservation laws.
2. **Link to Empirical Milestones**: State the exact empirical failure mode or experiment ID (`E####`) motivating the theoretical inquiry.
3. **Reference Foundational Literature**: Provide formal citations, author lists, and methodology links for peer-reviewed tracking and computer vision literature.
